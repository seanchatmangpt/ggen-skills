# ggen-first

Push repeatable knowledge upstream into the manufacturing layer.

## Use when

Prefer deterministic ggen-style manufacture and accumulated executable knowledge over repeated handwritten projection. Use when creating repeatable code, configuration, docs, tests, workflows, tool invocations, adapters, or skill surfaces.

## Inputs

- Contract and candidate outputs.
- Known marketplace patterns, generators, tools, and composition options.

## Procedure

1. Search for an admitted known manufacturing pattern and compose it.
2. If a specialized generator exists, manufacture its declarations, configuration, and invocation.
3. If a known tool sequence exists, manufacture the script/workflow.
4. Encode repo-specific differences as facts or parameters.
5. Author only irreducible novel mechanism.
6. After qualification, promote novelty into reusable manufacturing knowledge.

## Output

A manufacturing decision with chosen knowledge source, irreducible mechanism if any, and promotion path.

## Falsifiers and refusals

- Do not claim ggen manufacture without observed ggen execution.
- A native generator is capital equipment, not an excuse to move authority downstream.

## Composition

This skill may be composed by `aps-protocol`. Preserve explicit handoff state and do not infer authority across skill boundaries.
