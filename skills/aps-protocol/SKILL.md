# aps-protocol

Start from the exact subject and preserve its useful truth. Route work through the full protocol rather than jumping from request to actuation.

## Use when

Apply the complete APS knowledge-work reconstitution state machine. Use for complex implementation, migration, governance, or agent workflows that need admitted knowledge, DfCM, authority separation, receipts, replay, and bounded standing.

## Inputs

- Exact subject identity and requested consequence.
- Observed evidence and declared constraints.
- Available capability and authority surfaces.

## Procedure

1. Orient capabilities and authority.
2. Preserve behavior, contracts, evidence, and constraints before replacement.
3. Observe and classify facts; admit only bounded `O*`.
4. Define the contract and falsifiers.
5. Use DfCM to enumerate the reversible lawful frontier.
6. Perform `SELECT`, then `CONSTRUCT`, as distinct transitions.
7. Route any consequential `DO` through an explicit authority boundary.
8. Capture receipt and independently verify consequence.
9. Replay when required and assign bounded standing.
10. Promote reusable qualified knowledge; sunset stale implementation privilege.

## Output

A protocol trace containing observed, admitted, selected, constructed, executed, verified, inferred, refused, blocked, unsupported, and standing fields.

## Falsifiers and refusals

- Refuse any request to treat a plan or generated artifact as automatic DO authority.
- Do not crown `ALIVE` when execution against the exact admitted subject was not observed.

## Composition

This skill may be composed by `aps-protocol`. Preserve explicit handoff state and do not infer authority across skill boundaries.
