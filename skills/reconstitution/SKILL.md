# reconstitution

Everything may sink except truth that re-earns admission.

## Use when

Rebuild a useful capability from admitted truth rather than preserving incumbent structure. Use for greenfield successors, migrations, legacy replacement, or periodic architecture resets.

## Inputs

- Preservation ledger.
- Admitted contract and candidate frontier.
- Replacement acceptance boundary.

## Procedure

1. Recover useful behavior, interfaces, evidence, and constraints.
2. Grant no continuation privilege to incumbent structure.
3. Use DfCM to explore successor realizations.
4. Construct and qualify the successor against the recovered contract.
5. Prove containment/compatibility where required.
6. Sunset predecessor authority only after successor standing is sufficient.

## Output

A successor with evidence-linked correspondence from preserved truth to new consequence plus explicit predecessor sunset state.

## Falsifiers and refusals

- A rewrite that preserves only code shape is not reconstitution.
- Do not delete predecessor evidence needed for long-horizon reconstruction.

## Composition

This skill may be composed by `aps-protocol`. Preserve explicit handoff state and do not infer authority across skill boundaries.
