# skill-promoter

Convert novelty from one-off craft into inspectable capital.

## Use when

Promote a qualified novel mechanism, workflow, falsifier, or domain pattern into reusable skill/manufacturing knowledge. Use after repeated successful local solutions reveal stable reusable structure.

## Inputs

- Qualified local mechanism and receipts.
- Candidate reuse contexts and variation points.

## Procedure

1. Extract invariant knowledge from repo-specific details.
2. Identify parameters, contracts, and authority assumptions.
3. Choose skill, reference, script, template, or upstream ggen pattern as the reusable carrier.
4. Create adversarial reuse cases across distinct contexts.
5. Qualify the reusable form independently.
6. Deprecate duplicated local instructions only after coverage is proven.

## Output

A promoted reusable artifact with provenance to originating receipts and explicit applicability bounds.

## Falsifiers and refusals

- Do not promote an unqualified workaround.
- Do not generalize from one example without naming the inferred boundary.

## Composition

This skill may be composed by `aps-protocol`. Preserve explicit handoff state and do not infer authority across skill boundaries.
